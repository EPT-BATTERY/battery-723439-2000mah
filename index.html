Ⅱ.Waste Battery Management

1Description of Hazardous Substances

This battery does not contain mercury, cadmium or lead exceeding the regulatory limits. The alkaline electrolyte (potassium hydroxide) contained is corrosive and may cause irritation if leaked. Do not short-circuit, disassemble, puncture or throw it into the fire.

2Correct classification and placement guidelines

Do not discard in household garbage or mixed garbage

Please place the used batteries in the designated battery recycling bins or return them to the retailer's recycling points.
